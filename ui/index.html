🎙️ Physical Phone & Emulator Audio Setup

🔁 Routing Audio to/from the Phone

Because ADB/USB cannot inject microphone audio into a physical phone call natively, choose one of these setups:

Option A: Bluetooth (Wireless & Recommended)
  • Pair your physical phone to your PC via Bluetooth.
  • In the dropdowns above, select the Hands-Free AG Audio device for both input and output (e.g. [WASAPI] Headset (YourPhone Hands-Free)).
  • The PC acts as a headset. The AI speaks and hears the call wirelessly!
Option B: Android Emulator (Software-Only)
  • Run a dialer app (Linphone/WhatsApp/Skype) inside an Android emulator (LDPlayer, BlueStacks) on this PC.
  • Install VB-Cable on the PC. Select CABLE Input as Speaker and CABLE Output as Microphone.
  • Configure the emulator's Microphone setting to record from CABLE Output.
Option C: Physical AUX Cable & TRRS Splitter
  • Connect a 3.5mm TRRS auxiliary cable + splitter from the PC soundcard's headphone jack into the phone's headphone jack.
  • Select your physical PC Microphone/Speakers in the dropdowns.
🔊 How to hear the AI on PC while using Virtual Cable:

If using VB-Cable, you won't hear sound on your PC speaker because it goes to the virtual line. To hear it: Go to Windows Sound Control Panel → Recording Tab → Right-click CABLE Output → Properties → Listen Tab → Check "Listen to this device" and select your PC speakers/headphones.

📲 Android USB Setup Instructions

  1. Connect your Android phone to PC via USB cable.
  2. Open Settings > About Phone & tap Build Number 7 times.
  3. Go to Settings > System / Developer Options & turn ON USB Debugging.
  4. Check your phone screen for: "Allow USB Debugging?" → Check "Always allow" & tap OK.
  5. Click "Connect via USB" above or tap "Restart ADB Server" if stuck.
